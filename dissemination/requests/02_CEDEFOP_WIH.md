# Email 2 — Cedefop / Eurostat Web Intelligence Hub: Skills-OVATE microdata (France)

**To:** skills-ovate@cedefop.europa.eu (and the WIH team via ESTAT-WIH@ec.europa.eu)
**Subject:** Research access request — Skills-OVATE / WIH online job advertisement data for France, 2018–2026

Dear Skills-OVATE and WIH teams,

I am a researcher at the ELIA laboratory (employment × AI) of Paris School of Technology & Business and at GRETTIA-COSYS, Université Gustave Eiffel. With Armand Derhy (ELIA, PST&B) and Mahdi Zargayouna (Université Gustave Eiffel; ELIA, PST&B), we study the emergence, diffusion and lifecycle of artificial-intelligence occupations in France from online job advertisements, with a pre-registered protocol (ESCO-based skill extraction, an emergence index, validation on an annotated sample).

We have built an open corpus of more than one million French postings with full text (France Travail API, 2026), 1.6 million public-sector postings (2022–2026) and an international archive of 92,000 postings (2011–2026). Skills-OVATE would give us the only harmonised European benchmark and the 2018–2025 depth for France.

We would like to ask whether the following could be made available for research use, under the conditions you apply to academic partners:
1. the quarterly France aggregates at the finest level available (ISCO-08 unit group × ESCO skill × NUTS-2), 2018 Q3 to the latest quarter;
2. where possible, access to de-identified microdata (title, deduplicated text, date, NUTS-2, ISCO/ESCO codes, source website) for France, for a defined period, through the WIH secure environment;
3. the documentation of the deduplication and classification pipeline, to align our method with yours.

We commit to use the data for statistical and scientific purposes only, to publish aggregates only, and to credit Cedefop and Eurostat. Results will be submitted to a peer-reviewed journal and made public through an observatory website.

Thank you for considering this request; I am available for a call at your convenience.
