**To:** economicgraph@linkedin.com · same text submitted through the Economic Graph research-partnership web form
**Subject:** Research data request — aggregated France job-posting series by title and skill, 2019–2026 (ELIA, PST&B / Université Gustave Eiffel)

Dear Economic Graph research team,

I am a researcher at the ELIA laboratory (employment × AI) of Paris School of Technology & Business and at GRETTIA-COSYS, Université Gustave Eiffel. With Armand Derhy and Mahdi Zargayouna, we study how AI occupations emerge, spread and age in France from job postings (pre-registered lexical rules, Emergence Index per job title, LLM-judge validation).

Our open corpus holds 2.8 million postings (France Travail, public sector, Hacker News, Adzuna); to measure AI-titled and AI-related postings outside the public employment service, we need private-sector coverage. Your 2018 Economic Graph Research Program gave academics aggregated data in a secure sandbox; we would like to work in that spirit.

We ask for:
1. monthly aggregated counts of job postings in France, 2019–2026, by standardised title and skill (AI included), with region and industry splits if possible;
2. if feasible, sandbox access to run our title-level Emergence Index on these aggregates;
3. documentation of your title and AI-skill taxonomies.

In return: LinkedIn Economic Graph credited in our paper (submitted to Technological Forecasting and Social Change), observatory and monthly barometer; a co-authored note with your economists; early observatory access.

Data protection: no member-level or personal data requested; only aggregates published (counts below 10 suppressed); secure storage on university infrastructure; no redistribution.

I can present the protocol by call or complete your proposal form.
